Questions

Common questions about water damage

Do you really answer at night and on weekends?

Yes. Emergency restoration is 24/7, because water doesn’t wait for business hours. Call and you’ll reach us.

Will my insurance cover this?

It depends on your policy and what caused the leak. Call your insurer early and take photos before anything is moved. We document the damage and prepare a detailed estimate for your adjuster, and we can deal with your insurer directly.

How long does drying take?

It depends on how much water there was and what got wet. We monitor moisture as we go and only close up walls and floors once the structure is dry.

Will I get mold?

Water left in walls and floors is how mold starts, so we dry the structure properly and apply antimicrobial treatment. If mold is already there, we handle the removal too.

Can you rebuild afterwards?

Yes. Full rebuilds are part of what we do, from drywall and flooring to kitchens, with the same team managing it.

What areas do you cover?

Mississauga, Toronto and across the GTA, including Brampton, Vaughan, Markham, Oakville and Durham. Tell us where you are when you call.
